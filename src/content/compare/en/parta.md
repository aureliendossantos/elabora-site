## The difference in one minute

Parta calls itself a content orchestration platform. Courses are stored in its cloud; agents connected through its MCP draft and edit them, translate them with linked language versions, audit accessibility and bring in SCORM packages from other tools.

Elabora is a desktop app for one author and their AI. The AI proposes a plan, builds the screens in a folder on your computer, and each change can be reviewed and undone.

## Parta’s strengths

- **Multilingual courses** with linked language versions and Phrase TMS.
- **Team work**: real-time collaboration, roles, permissions and tasks from the Studio plan.
- **Layouts**: more than 150 content and quiz layouts, and your own templates.

## Where Elabora differs

Elabora was designed from the start for a reliable and pleasant experience, with your AI doing the building.

- **Your own AI does the work, on your files.** Parta’s agents draft, edit, translate and audit accessibility inside its platform (MCP 3.0, October 2026). In Elabora, your own AI does this work on folders you keep, and after each change it reads Elabora’s checks, on accessibility, learning design and your client’s LMS, and fixes what they find on its own.
- **Designed to measure, in less of your time.** Your AI designs each course for its audience, its look, illustrations and layouts included, then builds the screens from a plan you approve. Your time goes to the content and the choices.
- **Free**, MCP server included.
- **Files you keep**, in an open format.
- **Your own AI subscription.** Elabora works with the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already have, so you use AI as much as that plan allows, well beyond the AI course quotas of Parta’s plans. It does ask you to have that subscription; Elabora’s free chat covers smaller edits.
- **Richer screens**: 3D objects learners operate, dialogues with characters who speak, narration with free local voices.
