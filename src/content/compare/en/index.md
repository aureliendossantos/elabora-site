## 4 families of tools

**Desktop editors.** [Storyline 360](/en/compare/storyline/), [Adobe Captivate](/en/compare/captivate/), [iSpring Suite](/en/compare/ispring/) and [Lectora](/en/compare/lectora/) give you full control over each slide and deliver to most LMS standards. Storyline and iSpring run on Windows only; on a Mac, see [a Storyline alternative for Mac](/en/compare/storyline-alternative-mac/).

**Web tools for teams.** [Rise 360](/en/compare/rise/), [dominKnow | ONE](/en/compare/dominknow/), [Easygenerator](/en/compare/easygenerator/), [Create (formerly Elucidat)](/en/compare/elucidat/) and [Gomo](/en/compare/gomo/) run in the browser, with co-authoring, roles and review for organisations that produce many courses.

**Visual tools.** [Genially](/en/compare/genially/) makes interactive presentations, games and infographics, with SCORM on its Author plan.

**AI-first tools.** [Mindsmith](/en/compare/mindsmith/) and [Parta](/en/compare/parta/) are web apps where an AI agent drafts courses, and where your own AI can connect through MCP.

## Where Elabora fits

Elabora is a free desktop app for macOS and Windows. Your AI, in the Claude app, the ChatGPT app or Elabora’s free chat, proposes a plan of objectives, parts and screens; you approve it; it then designs the course to measure, its look, illustrations and layouts included, and builds the screens, which you refine by asking or by editing each block.

You save the time of building, with peace of mind: after each change, your AI reads Elabora’s checks, on accessibility, learning design and your client’s LMS, and fixes what they find on its own. Each course is a folder of files in an open format, delivered as a package for your LMS (SCORM 1.2 or 2004, cmi5 or xAPI) or a web link.

Its learning-design checks apply research on how people learn. Richard Mayer’s principles of multimedia learning keep cognitive load in check: short segments, bounded text, narration and on-screen text that complement each other. Learners get something to do every few screens, feedback that explains each answer, objectives they can show, and spaced retrieval planned after the course.

It suits a designer or a small team who delivers courses to an LMS or on the web and wants rich screens, 3D scenes, dialogues, custom interactions and narration, built with AI. It also suits an employee who runs training without being an instructional designer: the checks guide the course’s design. For a team editing the same course, the tools above do it today.
