## The difference in one minute

Gomo is built for production teams: authors, editors and reviewers work in the browser, feedback on each screen becomes a task, and live delivery updates courses in the LMS without a new upload. It is sold on quote, by company size.

Elabora is a desktop app for one author and their AI: the AI proposes a plan, builds the screens, and every change can be reviewed and undone.

## Gomo’s strengths

- **A production workflow**: roles, real-time co-authoring with topic locking, and review feedback that becomes tasks.
- **Languages**: more than 250, with AI translation and XLIFF.
- **Live delivery**: updates reach the LMS without uploading the course again.

## Where Elabora differs

Elabora was designed from the start for a reliable and pleasant experience, with your AI doing the building.

- **Free**, and installed in minutes.
- **Designed to measure, in less of your time.** Your AI designs each course for its audience, its look, illustrations and layouts included, then builds the screens from a plan you approve. Your time goes to the content and the choices. The Claude app, the ChatGPT app or a free chat does the work.
- **Peace of mind.** After each change, your AI reads Elabora’s checks, on accessibility, learning design and your client’s LMS, and fixes what they find on its own, often before you notice.
- **Scenes and conversations**: 3D objects, dialogues with characters, narration with free local voices.
