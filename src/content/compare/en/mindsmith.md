## The difference in one minute

Mindsmith is a web app. Its agent turns your request and files into a storyboard, then a lesson; your team edits it live and shares it as a link or a SCORM package that can update itself in the LMS. AI work uses credits, included in each plan.

Elabora is a desktop app. Your AI, in the Claude app, the ChatGPT app or Elabora’s free chat, proposes a plan and builds the screens in a folder on your computer. Each change becomes a card you review, and one click undoes it.

## Mindsmith’s strengths

- **Live co-editing** on Business and Enterprise.
- **Dynamic SCORM**: the package in the LMS shows the version you last published.
- **PDF**: a lesson also exports as a PDF, besides SCORM and HTML.
- **An MCP server that publishes**, and that reads learner results for your AI.
- **Accessibility**: its learner side is built to meet WCAG 2.2 AA, with a VPAT on request.

## Where Elabora differs

Elabora was designed from the start for a reliable and pleasant experience, with your AI doing the building.

- **Designed to measure, in less of your time.** Your AI designs each course for its audience, its look, illustrations and layouts included, then builds the screens from a plan you approve. Your time goes to the content and the choices.
- **Peace of mind.** After each change, your AI reads Elabora’s checks, on accessibility, learning design and your client’s LMS, and fixes what they find on its own, often before you notice.
- **Files you keep.** Mindsmith lessons are stored in your Mindsmith workspace; an Elabora course is a folder in an open format, readable without Elabora.
- **Your own AI subscription.** Elabora works with the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already have, so you use AI as much as that plan allows, with no credits to count. It does ask you to have that subscription; Elabora’s free chat covers smaller edits.
- **Richer screens**: 3D objects learners operate, dialogues with characters who speak, narration with free local voices or ElevenLabs.
- **Free on your computer**, with free reviews. Online, each author has free quotas to create review links and publish courses on the web.
