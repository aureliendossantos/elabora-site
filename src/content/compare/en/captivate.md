## The difference in one minute

Captivate is a full desktop editor. You lay out responsive slides, record software, add interactions and publish to most LMS standards. Since version 13, it also generates images with Adobe Firefly and rewrites texts.

Elabora works from what your learners must be able to do. The AI proposes a plan, you approve it, then it builds the screens; you refine them with the agent or by hand.

## Captivate’s strengths

- **Software simulations**, a long-standing strength, and Captivate Classic simulation projects can now be brought into the new version (beta).
- **A certified accessibility level.** Adobe certifies version 13.2 for WCAG 2.2 AA and publishes its conformance report.
- **Adobe’s ecosystem**: Firefly images, and team licences managed in Adobe’s admin console.

## Where Elabora differs

Elabora was designed from the start for a reliable and pleasant experience, with your AI doing the building.

- **Designed to measure, in less of your time.** Your AI designs each course for its audience, its look, illustrations and layouts included, then builds the screens from a plan you approve. Your time goes to the content and the choices. Each change becomes a card you review, and one click undoes it.
- **Your own AI subscription.** Elabora works with the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already have, so you use AI as much as that plan allows, well beyond the generative credits Adobe includes. It does ask you to have that subscription; Elabora’s free chat covers smaller edits.
- **Interactions of any kind.** When a screen needs more than the built-in blocks, your AI writes a free object in JavaScript: a 3D simulation, a mini-game or a custom interaction, sandboxed, tested, and settable by you without code.
- **Free, with your files.** Elabora costs nothing on your computer, and each course is a folder in an open format.
- **Scenes and conversations.** 3D objects learners operate, and dialogues where characters speak and learners choose their answers.
- **Reviews down to the word.** Reviews are free: your reviewers comment from a link, without an account, and their comments are placed on the block or the word of narration.
- **Peace of mind.** After each change, your AI reads Elabora’s checks and fixes what they find on its own, often before you notice: accessibility (contrast, text alternatives, keyboard use, captions), learning design, and the rules of your client’s LMS, which each package is tested against in an LMS emulator.
