## The difference in one minute

iSpring Suite is a PowerPoint add-in. You build slides in PowerPoint, then add quizzes, dialogue simulations, narration and screen recordings, and publish. Your course is a presentation.

Elabora is a separate app. You describe the course and attach your documents, PowerPoint files included; the AI proposes a plan, then builds the screens in Elabora’s format.

## iSpring’s strengths

- **The PowerPoint workflow.** Authors keep the tool they know, with its animations and layouts.
- **MP4 video.** iSpring also turns a course into an MP4 video.
- **Ready-made content.** A Content Library of more than 130,000 assets.
- **Languages.** AI translation into more than 70 languages and text-to-speech in 53 (Suite AI and Cloud AI).

## Where Elabora differs

Elabora was designed from the start for a reliable and pleasant experience, with your AI doing the building.

- **On Mac and Windows**, as a desktop app, with no PowerPoint to install.
- **Designed to measure, in less of your time.** Your AI designs each course for its audience, its look, illustrations and layouts included, then builds the screens from a plan you approve. Your time goes to the content and the choices. The Claude app, the ChatGPT app on your computer or Elabora’s free chat does the work. Each change can be reviewed and undone.
- **Peace of mind.** After each change, your AI reads Elabora’s checks, on accessibility, learning design and your client’s LMS, and fixes what they find on its own, often before you notice.
- **Your own AI subscription.** Elabora works with the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already have, so you use AI as much as that plan allows, with no AI plan to add. It does ask you to have that subscription; Elabora’s free chat covers smaller edits.
- **Beyond slides.** Courses can be slides or a scrolling page, with 3D scenes and dialogues where characters speak.
- **Free, with files you keep**, in an open format.
