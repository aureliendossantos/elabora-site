## The difference in one minute

Create, formerly Elucidat, serves L&D teams in large organisations: templates with locked brand and styles, automatic translation into more than 75 languages, and updates that reach learners without a new upload. Pricing is on quote.

Elabora is a desktop app for one author and their AI: the AI proposes a plan, builds the screens, and you refine them.

## Create’s strengths

- **Scale.** Templates with brand and style locking, reuse across projects, and user management across teams.
- **Translation.** Automatic translation into more than 75 languages, bulk translation, glossaries, XLIFF, CSV and JSON export.
- **Updates in place.** Changes reach learners without uploading the SCORM package again.

## Where Elabora differs

Elabora was designed from the start for a reliable and pleasant experience, with your AI doing the building.

- **Designed to measure, in less of your time.** Your AI designs each course for its audience, its look, illustrations and layouts included, then builds the screens from a plan you approve. Your time goes to the content and the choices.
- **Peace of mind.** After each change, your AI reads Elabora’s checks, on accessibility, learning design and your client’s LMS, and fixes what they find on its own, often before you notice.
- **Free**, installed in minutes, with no sales call.
- **Your choice of AI**, working on files you keep, in an open format.
- **Scenes and conversations**: 3D objects learners operate, dialogues with characters who speak.
