## The difference in one minute

Lectora is a classic authoring tool with an accessibility checker, in a desktop version for Windows and an online version for the browser. ELB Learning sells it in a suite with ReviewLink, an asset library and MicroBuilder.

Elabora starts from a brief. Your AI proposes a plan, you approve it, then it builds the screens, which you refine yourself or through the agent.

## Lectora’s strengths

- **Review at scale.** ReviewLink has unlimited free reviewers, with statuses and priorities on comments.
- **Assets.** The suite’s Asset Library holds more than 160 million stock assets and thousands of templates.
- **Two ways to work**: the desktop app on Windows, and Lectora Online in a browser.

## Where Elabora differs

Elabora was designed from the start for a reliable and pleasant experience, with your AI doing the building.

- **Designed to measure, in less of your time.** Your AI designs each course for its audience, its look, illustrations and layouts included, then builds the screens from a plan you approve. Your time goes to the content and the choices. Each change can be reviewed and undone.
- **A desktop app on Mac and Windows**, free, with your courses as folders in an open format.
- **Scenes and conversations**: 3D objects learners operate, dialogues with characters who speak, narration with free local voices or ElevenLabs.
- **Peace of mind.** After each change, your AI reads Elabora’s checks and fixes what they find on its own, often before you notice: accessibility (contrast, text alternatives, keyboard use, captions), learning design, and the rules of your client’s LMS, which each package is tested against in an LMS emulator.
