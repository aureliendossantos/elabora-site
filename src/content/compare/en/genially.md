## The difference in one minute

Genially is a visual canvas in the browser. You start from one of its many templates and animate it: buttons, reveals, games, quizzes. It is quick, playful and made for co-editing.

Elabora starts from what learners must be able to do. The AI proposes a plan of objectives, parts and screens, then writes them; quizzes give feedback on each answer, and the package goes to your LMS.

## Genially’s strengths

- **Visuals and games**: interactive images, quizzes, games and escape rooms, from more than 1,000 templates.
- **Co-editing** in real time with no limit on collaborators.
- **LTI 1.3** on the Author plan, to connect a course to several LMSs.

## Where Elabora differs

Elabora was designed from the start for a reliable and pleasant experience, with your AI doing the building.

- **Designed to measure, in less of your time.** Your AI designs each course for its audience, its look, illustrations and layouts included, then builds the screens from a plan you approve. Your time goes to the content and the choices.
- **Peace of mind.** After each change, your AI reads Elabora’s checks, on accessibility, learning design and your client’s LMS, and fixes what they find on its own, often before you notice.
- **A course, from a plan.** The AI drafts objectives, parts and screens, and each package comes with a report of how the screens cover the plan.
- **Free, packages included.** Genially starts with a free plan, and SCORM comes with its Author plan. Elabora is free on your computer, with SCORM, cmi5 and xAPI packages tested against the rules of common LMSs.
- **Your files and your AI.** A folder in an open format, built by the Claude app, the ChatGPT app or a free chat.
- **Your own AI subscription.** Elabora works with the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already have, so you use AI as much as that plan allows, well beyond the AI credits of Genially’s plans. It does ask you to have that subscription; Elabora’s free chat covers smaller edits.
