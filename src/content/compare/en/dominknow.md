## The difference in one minute

dominKnow | ONE is a content platform. Authors build courses in 2 modes, Claro for fixed slides and Flow for responsive pages, share topics and elements across projects, and review them in the same place.

Elabora is a desktop app for one author and their AI. The AI proposes a plan, builds the screens, and each change can be undone.

## dominKnow’s strengths

- **Reuse.** One source for pages and elements used in several courses.
- **Team work.** Real-time work in shared projects, roles and group permissions, version history with rollback.
- **Review built in.** Comments on a page or on one element, review periods with notifications, comments tracked until they are dealt with.
- **On-premises hosting** for Enterprise.

## Where Elabora differs

- **Free on your computer**, where dominKnow is sold on quote, per author.
- **Designed to measure, in less of your time.** Your AI designs each course for its audience, its look, illustrations and layouts included, then builds the screens from a plan you approve. Your time goes to the content and the choices. Claude, ChatGPT or Elabora’s free chat does the work, on your files.
- **Peace of mind.** After each change, your AI reads Elabora’s checks, on accessibility, learning design and your client’s LMS, and fixes what they find on its own, often before you notice.
- **Scenes and conversations**: 3D objects learners operate, dialogues with characters who speak.
- **Your files**, as folders in an open format.
