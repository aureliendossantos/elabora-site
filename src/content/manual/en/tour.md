## The home page

The home page opens on **My courses**.

- **Library**: your courses, each with its first screen as a thumbnail and the date it was modified. A card can also say “2 issues to fix” (red checks) or “3 comments to handle” (remarks from your reviewers). A click opens the course; the card’s **…** menu offers **Export…** and **Delete**.
- **Examples**: example courses that come with Elabora. A click opens your own copy, which you can change without fear; **Start again from the example**, in its **…** menu, makes a fresh one.
- Top right: **Import…** (a course from elsewhere) and **New course**.
- In the left column: **Home**, the **Trash** and, at the very bottom, the **You** menu: **Sign in to Elabora…** (or **Account…** once signed in), **Your preferred AI…** and **Settings…**.

Under the courses, a note reminds you that Elabora is in beta, and the **Coming in the next updates** box says what is on its way.

## A course’s window

At the top, the course’s bar:

- the house goes back **Home**;
- the course title opens a menu: **Course settings**, **Theme**, **Translation**, **Export…** (and **Import report** for a course imported from Storyline or Rise);
- the check mark next to the title says “Saved on this computer”: Elabora saves as you go, there is no save button. Clicking it opens the **Course history**;
- in the middle, the five views;
- on the right, **Preview**, which plays the course in its own window.

| View | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Course plan** | The course plan, its versions and your client’s approval. See [the course plan](/en/docs/course-plan/). |
| **Storyboard** | Every screen as a thumbnail, by chapter. Drag a thumbnail to move its screen; double-click to open it in Slide. |
| **Slide** | Where you see and retouch the screens. See [screens and blocks](/en/docs/screens-and-blocks/). |
| **Review** | Your reviewers’ comments, and the contact sheet of narrated screens. See [online reviews](/en/docs/online-reviews/). |
| **Publish** | The SCORM package, the web version, the review link, and the **Checks**. See [publishing](/en/docs/publishing/). |

## Slide, from left to right

- The list of screens, by chapter. ⌘B hides or shows it. When the window is narrow, it folds away by itself: bring the pointer to the left edge to slide it back in.
- The canvas: the screen as learners will see it. Above it, the layer tabs when the screen has layers; below it, the narration drawer (**Script** and **Timeline**).
- The toolbar: **Text**, **Image**, **Media**, **Question** and **Course blocks** to add a block; undo and redo; the zoom percentage, whose menu offers **Fit to window**, **Phone width** and **Reading order**; the magnifier to search the course.
- The right panel: **Properties** (the selected block or screen), **Agent** (your AI and what it did) and, when you are signed in, **Comments**. ⌥⌘B hides or shows it.

When the window gets narrower, the tools keep only their icons; at half a screen (next to your AI, for instance), the right panel floats over the canvas and a strip of thumbnails appears below it.

## Handy shortcuts

On Windows, use Ctrl for ⌘ and Alt for ⌥.

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z | Undo / redo (everything the agent did in one batch counts as one step) |
| ⌘F | Search the course (in Course plan: search the plan) |
| ⌘B / ⌥⌘B | Hide or show the list / the right panel |
| Double-click, or Enter | Edit the selected text; Escape leaves without changing anything |
| ⌘D, ⌫ | Duplicate, delete the selected block |
| ⌥↑, ⌥↓ | Move the selected block up, down |
| ⌘C, ⌘V | Copy the block, paste it after the selected block, on any screen |
| ⌘, | Elabora settings |

Every change, yours or the agent’s, is one step that ⌘Z undoes. To go further back, use the [history](/en/docs/library/).
