Elabora is a desktop app for Mac and Windows, made for people who produce e-learning courses: freelancers and small studios.

You work in it with your own AI. Your agent (Claude Code in the Claude app, Codex in the ChatGPT app, or any other AI you paste the request into) writes the course plan and the screens. Elabora shows you the screens exactly as your learners will see them, lets you retouch them by hand and undo what the agent did. It keeps track of the plan your client approved, collects your reviewers’ comments through a simple link, and delivers a SCORM 1.2 package for your client’s LMS, or a web version.

Elabora is in beta: the app changes quickly, and some features are marked **Beta**. Your feedback is welcome at contact@elabora.studio.

## The life of a course

1. You describe the course. On the **New course** page, you write your request and attach your client’s documents (a brief, a storyboard, a PowerPoint mockup, recordings). You can also start from a Storyline or Rise course.
2. The agent writes the course plan. You read it, make a version of it (V1), send it to your client as Word or PDF, then record their approval.
3. The agent writes the screens. You watch them being built and retouch them by hand: text, images, the order of blocks, interactions, voices.
4. You have it reviewed. **Preview** plays the course as an LMS would, **Checks** list what is left to fix, and a review link lets your client comment online, without an account.
5. You deliver. A package in the format your client’s LMS expects, in one language or several, or a publication on the web.

Each page answers a practical question (“how do I…”). Names in bold are the ones you see in the app. When something is done by asking your agent rather than with a button, the page says so and gives an example request.

Elabora’s interface is in English or French. It follows your system’s language preferences, or the language you choose in **Settings**. This manual exists in both languages.
