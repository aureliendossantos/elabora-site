## What the extension gives you

- **Your courses, in Claude’s chat.** No terminal and no folder to choose: Claude sees the courses of your library and those opened from elsewhere in Elabora. It reads and writes their plan, screens and blocks, checks them, looks at a screen as a learner sees it and builds the SCORM package, with about a hundred tools Elabora provides.
- **The guidance of your Elabora version.** The extension contains no copy of Elabora: it starts the Elabora app installed on your computer, with its rules and its guides for each task. When Elabora updates, Claude follows.
- **Your hand on everything.** Elabora shows what Claude changes as it happens, and undoes it in one step. See [Working with your AI](/en/docs/working-with-your-ai/).

With Claude Code, nothing to install: see [Elabora and Claude Code](/en/docs/claude-code/).

## Before you start

- [Elabora](/en/docs/install/), installed and opened once. On a Mac, in the Applications folder.
- The [Claude](https://claude.com/download) desktop app, on a Mac or on Windows.

## Installing the extension

1. In Elabora: **You** › **Settings…** › **Tools for AI** › **Install in Claude**. The button shows when the Claude app is on your computer. (Or [download the extension](/downloads/elabora.mcpb), `elabora.mcpb`, and double-click it.)
2. Claude opens and shows Elabora: install it. Claude may warn that the extension does not come from its directory: Elabora’s extension is installed from its file.
3. In a new conversation, the tools menu lists Elabora.

A course created or opened in Elabora later shows up too, with nothing to reinstall.

## Three requests to try

> Which Elabora courses can you work on? Give me an overview of each one.

> In the coffee course, on the brewing page, add a quick question about the brewing ratio, with feedback for each answer.

> Check the coffee course and tell me what to fix before I send it to the client.

With several courses, name the one you mean in your request.

## Try it on a sample course

[Download the sample course](/downloads/coffee-in-brief.elabora) “From cherry to cup: coffee in brief”: a 15-minute course for the new baristas of a fictional café chain, which uses every starter block of Elabora. Double-click the file: Elabora adds it to your library, and Claude sees it.

## If something goes wrong

- **Claude says Elabora was not found**: on a Mac, Elabora must be in the Applications folder (yours or the computer’s); on Windows, installed with its installer. Open Elabora once, then restart Claude.
- **Claude says Elabora has not been opened yet**: open Elabora once. Claude then sees your courses, with no restart.
- **Claude offers only Elabora’s documentation**: update Elabora. The extension needs a version more recent than 0.4.0.
